Privacy by design

Encrypted before it ever leaves you

Not password-protected-after-upload. Encrypted-first, always. If someone seized the server, they'd get noise.

1 · You write

Notes are encrypted on your device with your password as the key. Plaintext never travels.

→
2 · Server stores noise

The server sees only ciphertext. No words, no tags, no titles to mine or subpoena.

→
3 · Only you unlock

Login unlocks locally. Recovery codes unwrap your key — only you can regain access.
